== Baseline self-test (SAT)

For a gradeable "where am I" test your dad can score by hand, use the *linear paper practice tests (Tests 4–11)* — each PDF bundles the answer key and raw-to-scaled scoring chart. Take it timed on paper, then convert. Switch to Bluebook afterward for realistic (adaptive) practice.

* Download: https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/practice/practice-tests/paper[satsuite.collegeboard.org/practice/practice-tests/paper]
* Use *Tests 4–11 only* — the older paper tests (1–10) are the retired pre-2024 format and won't map to current scoring.

== Notes

* *Confirmed fees:* SAT $68, AP $99, CLEP $97 + center fee.
* *Verify before paying:* ACT core (~$65) and PSAT (~$18) shift slightly and by add-on — check act.org / the host school.
* *Fee waivers* exist for SAT/AP (College Board) and ACT for eligible low-income 11th–12th graders; they cover base fees, late fees, and extra score reports.
* You register first; the *test center* (a nearby high school) is chosen inside the registration flow and appears on your admission ticket. Register early to get a close one.
